Pricing questions

Before we talk numbers.

Why are fixed prices not shown?

A meaningful price depends on the actual scope, content, integrations, timeline, and collaboration required. A short discovery conversation produces a more honest estimate.

Can a project be split into phases?

Yes. Phasing can prioritise the most valuable foundation first and create clear points for review, learning, and future investment.

What affects the quote most?

Scope, technical complexity, content readiness, custom integrations, the number of unique experiences, and delivery timing usually have the greatest impact.

Is ongoing support required?

No. We can hand over a documented system, provide ad-hoc support, or continue as an ongoing partner.
