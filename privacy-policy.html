Effective and last updated: 10 November 2026. ALIFY · Worldwide remote digital agency · hello@alify.com

Information we collect

ALIFY is a worldwide remote digital agency. We collect the contact, company, project, and communication details you submit through our website or send to hello@alify.com. If you attach a brief, we store the file privately with the inquiry. Our server may process IP address, request time, browser information, and security logs to operate and protect the website.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver services, maintain security, prevent abuse, meet accounting or legal obligations, and improve our services. We do not sell personal information or use project attachments for advertising.

Legal bases and retention

Depending on jurisdiction, processing is based on consent, steps requested before a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, or legal obligations. Unsuccessful inquiries and attachments are retained for up to 24 months; active client records may be retained for the engagement and up to 7 years where financial or contractual records are required; security logs are normally retained for up to 90 days.

Sharing and processors

Information may be processed by the hosting, email, cloud storage, accounting, analytics, or professional-advice providers used to operate ALIFY. Providers receive only information required for their role. International processing is protected through appropriate contractual and security safeguards where required.

Your rights and choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable law. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Browser controls can limit local storage; this may affect saved form drafts.

Contact and updates

For a privacy request, email hello@alify.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may verify your identity before completing a request. Material changes to this notice will be published on this page with a revised date.
