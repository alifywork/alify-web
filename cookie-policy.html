What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website. Local and session storage provide similar browser-based storage. ALIFY currently uses browser storage for interface preferences and optional form drafts rather than advertising profiles.

Essential and security processing

The website and inquiry API require standard server processing to deliver pages, prevent abuse, validate submissions, and protect stored inquiries. These functions do not require an advertising cookie banner. If non-essential analytics is enabled later, consent will be requested where applicable before activation.

Current interface storage

The mobile project prompt uses session storage to remember dismissal during the browsing session. Contact and Start Project save non-file draft fields in local storage on your device until you clear them or successfully submit. Attachments and privacy-consent state are not included in browser drafts.

Managing your choices

Use your browser settings to delete cookies and site data or block storage. You can clear an ALIFY draft by clearing site data for this website. Blocking essential storage may affect interface preferences and draft restoration but does not prevent normal email contact.

Third-party services

The current site self-hosts its fonts, icons, scripts, and visual assets. No advertising or social-media tracking pixels are intentionally loaded. Future embedded media, analytics, or externally hosted services will be reflected here before activation.

Updates and contact

Questions about browser storage can be sent to hello@alify.com. We will update this notice when the website’s actual storage or service providers change.
