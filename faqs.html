Services and capabilities

What we can help with.

What services does ALIFY provide?

Web development, SEO, digital marketing, AI automation, UI UX design, and logo design. These capabilities can be combined into one connected engagement.

Can you improve an existing digital product?

Yes. We begin by understanding what is working, where the friction is, and whether focused improvements or a broader redesign is the better investment.

Do you work with early-stage ideas?

Yes. We can help shape positioning, identity, an MVP experience, and a practical digital foundation for launch.

Process and collaboration

How the work moves forward.

What happens at the start of a project?

We align on the challenge, goals, audience, scope, responsibilities, timing, and success measures before creative production begins.

How often will we see progress?

Review points are agreed in the project plan. We prefer tangible progress and focused feedback over status meetings without decisions.

What do you need from our team?

Access to relevant context, timely feedback, a clear decision-maker, and any existing content or technical information needed for the scope.

Timing and pricing

Planning the practical details.

How long does a typical project take?

Timing varies with scope and dependencies. After discovery we provide a schedule based on real deliverables, integrations, content readiness, and review availability.

How much should we budget?

Share a comfortable range if you can. It helps us recommend the most useful scope and discuss trade-offs openly.

Can work be prioritised around a launch date?

Yes. We can define a minimum viable first release and phase additional features or content around the agreed deadline.

Support and ownership

What happens after delivery.

Will we be able to update the website?

Where a content-managed solution is included, we provide an appropriate editing experience and handover guidance.

Who owns the final work?

Ownership and licensing are set out in the project agreement, including any third-party assets, fonts, software, or subscriptions.

Can you support us after launch?

Yes. Ongoing support can cover optimisation, new features, content, campaigns, maintenance, or periodic creative reviews.
