# Pricing research — 10 November 2026

ALIFY displays indicative USD starting points and example budgets, not guaranteed quotations. Published market guides reviewed for comparable agency pricing:

- [FlowRush: US web design agency rates](https://flowrushtech.com/usa/best-website-development-agency-usa/) — small-agency business websites commonly $6,000–$25,000 and e-commerce $5,000–$25,000, with lower-cost published packages also present.
- [360 Digital Growth: agency cost guide](https://360digitalgrowth.com/blogs/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-web-design-agency-in-2026/) — basic business websites $1,500–$5,000, professional websites $3,000–$10,000, e-commerce $5,000–$30,000+.
- [Lifted Websites pricing](https://liftedwebsites.com/pricing/) — published website management, one-time build, local SEO and e-commerce SEO pricing.

ALIFY's US$2,500 project starting point is deliberately below many US agency averages to reflect worldwide remote delivery. Pricing is scope-dependent and excludes third-party costs unless explicitly included.
